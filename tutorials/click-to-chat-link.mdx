---\ntitle: "How to Create a WhatsApp Click-to-Chat Link"\ndescription: "Generate links to drive traffic to your WhatsApp."\n---\n\nUse the format `https://wa.me/YOUR_NUMBER?text=YOUR_URL_ENCODED_MESSAGE`. Place this link in your Instagram bio, Facebook page, or email footers. When users click it, it opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message ready to send to your OTP Edge bot.\n

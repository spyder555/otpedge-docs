---\ntitle: "How to Apply for the WhatsApp Blue Tick"\ndescription: "Get the Official Business Account (OBA) badge for your brand."\n---\n\nThe Green/Blue verification badge proves your brand's authenticity. To get it, your business must be highly notable. Go to your Meta Business Manager, navigate to WhatsApp Manager, and click **Profile > Submit for Verification**. You will need to provide 3-5 links to prominent press articles about your company.\n

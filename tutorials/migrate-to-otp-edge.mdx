---\ntitle: "How to Migrate to OTP Edge from Another Provider"\ndescription: "Port your WhatsApp number without downtime."\n---\n\nTo migrate, you must disable 2FA on your current provider (e.g., Twilio). Then, in OTP Edge, go to **Integrations > Connect Custom WABA**. Follow the embedded signup flow and enter your number. Meta will send an SMS OTP to verify ownership, and the migration happens instantly.\n

## The OTP Edge Flow

1.  **Dispatch**: Hit our `/v1/send-otp` endpoint with a phone number.
2.  **Verify**: Your user enters the code; you call `/v1/verify-otp`.
3.  **Identity**: Receive a signed JWT. Verify it offline on your backend.
